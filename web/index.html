Chroma Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

English - Español

The short version. Chroma turns one photo a day into one color, and your year of colors never leaves your phone. There is no account and no server: your photos, your colors, your words and your settings are stored only on your device and are never uploaded to us. The only data that leaves your device is what is needed to know whether you have bought Chroma Pro. There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind.

Who is responsible

Chroma ("the app") is published by Baltasar Jiménez ("we", "us"), an individual developer based in Spain, who acts as data controller for the limited processing described below.

Contact for anything in this policy: baltajmn@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Everything you create in Chroma is stored locally, in the app's private storage:

  • Your days: the date, the color you picked, the other colors the photo offered, the name of the color, and the optional word you added.
  • The photo of each day, as a reduced copy. The copy is made again from the pixels, so it carries none of the original file's metadata: no location, no camera details.
  • Your settings: reminder time, whether shared cards carry the "Chroma" mark, the date of your last backup, and whether you have purchased Chroma Pro.

The home screen widgets read a small separate file with today's color and its name and, with Chroma Pro, the colors of this year's days. It never contains your photos or your words.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. We have no server, so we could not receive it even if we wanted to. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

The camera and your photos

To take the photo of the day, Chroma opens your phone's camera; the app receives only the picture you take. To use a photo you already have, the system photo picker hands the app only the photo you choose, and never the rest of your library. From that photo the app reads the date it was taken, to check that it is from today, and nothing else.

Your phone's own backup

Your phone may include Chroma in the backup it makes to your own cloud account. On Android, Chroma only allows its file of colors into Google's backup when that backup is end-to-end encrypted, which requires a screen lock on your phone; photos are left out of the cloud backup and only travel when you transfer directly from one phone to another. On iPhone and iPad, the app's data is part of your device's iCloud Backup if you have it turned on. Those backups belong to your Google or Apple account and are covered by their policies. We cannot read them.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, the notification is created on your device and always says the same thing: it asks what color today is. It never shows your photos, your colors or your words.

What leaves your device

Purchases

Chroma offers a single one-time in-app purchase. Payments are processed by the store you installed Chroma from, Google Play or the App Store; we never see or receive your card details, billing address or any other payment information.

To know whether a given installation has bought Chroma Pro, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc. as a processor. RevenueCat assigns each installation an anonymous identifier and stores, on our behalf, that identifier, the purchase history associated with it, and technical device identifiers. Because Chroma has no accounts, this identifier is not linked to your name, your email address or any profile. Nothing from your photos or your colors is ever sent to RevenueCat. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

RevenueCat processes data in the United States. Transfers outside the European Economic Area are covered by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Nothing else

Chroma contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting SDK. We do not build profiles, we do not use remarketing, and we do not sell or share personal information with anyone. The app needs an internet connection only to check your purchase status.

Files you export and cards you share

Chroma can export all your days as one file that includes your colors, words and photos. Cards and posters are images made on your device, and a card can be shared with the photo or with the color only. You choose where a file is saved or where an image is sent. Once saved or sent, it is outside our control, and if it goes to a cloud folder or to another app, that provider's policy applies to it, not this one.

How long data is kept

Data on your device is kept until you delete a day or uninstall the app. Purchase records held by RevenueCat and by the app store are kept for as long as those services require in order to honour restores and to meet their own legal and tax obligations.

Children

Chroma is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. This version of the app has no accounts, no public content and no social features.

Your rights under the GDPR

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and object to the processing of your personal data, and the right to data portability.

In practice, most of these you can exercise yourself and instantly: the data is on your phone. Deleting a day erases it; uninstalling the app erases everything; the export feature is data portability. For the purchase data held by RevenueCat, write to baltajmn@gmail.com and we will act on your request. We can only identify your records if you provide the Google Play or App Store order number, precisely because we hold nothing that identifies you.

Legal bases: performance of a contract (delivering the purchase you made) and our legitimate interest in preventing fraudulent restores. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority. In Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos.

Your rights under the CCPA / CPRA

If you are a California resident: in the last twelve months we have collected the categories "identifiers" (an anonymous installation identifier and device identifiers) and "commercial information" (your purchase history), for the sole purpose of delivering the product you bought.

We have not sold or shared personal information, and we do not use it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We do not knowingly sell the personal information of anyone under 16. You have the right to know, to delete, to correct and to non-discrimination for exercising those rights; write to baltajmn@gmail.com.

Do Not Track

Chroma does not track you across apps or websites, so there is no tracking behaviour to change in response to a Do Not Track signal.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date at the top, and material changes will also be described in the app's release notes in the store. If a future version adds optional features to share with friends, this policy will be updated before that version is published, and nothing will leave your phone unless you turn those features on.

English - Español

En corto. Chroma convierte una foto al día en un color, y tu año de colores no sale de tu móvil. No hay cuenta ni servidor: tus fotos, tus colores, tus palabras y tus ajustes se guardan solo en tu dispositivo y no se nos envían nunca. Lo único que sale de tu móvil es lo necesario para saber si has comprado Chroma Pro. No hay publicidad, ni analítica, ni seguimiento de ningún tipo.

Quién es el responsable

Chroma ("la app") está publicada por Baltasar Jiménez ("nosotros"), desarrollador individual con residencia en España, que actúa como responsable del tratamiento limitado que se describe abajo.

Contacto para cualquier asunto de esta política: baltajmn@gmail.com.

Lo que se queda en tu dispositivo

Todo lo que creas en Chroma se guarda en local, en el almacenamiento privado de la app:

  • Tus días: la fecha, el color que elegiste, los otros colores que ofrecía la foto, el nombre del color y la palabra opcional que añadiste.
  • La foto de cada día, como copia reducida. La copia se hace de nuevo a partir de los píxeles, así que no lleva ninguno de los metadatos del fichero original: ni ubicación, ni datos de la cámara.
  • Tus ajustes: hora del recordatorio, si las tarjetas llevan la marca "Chroma", la fecha de tu última copia y si has comprado Chroma Pro.

Los widgets de inicio leen un fichero aparte, pequeño, con el color de hoy y su nombre y, con Chroma Pro, los colores de los días de este año. Nunca contiene tus fotos ni tus palabras.

Nada de esto se nos envía ni se envía a terceros. No tenemos servidor, así que no podríamos recibirlo aunque quisiéramos. Desinstalar la app lo borra.

La cámara y tus fotos

Para hacer la foto del día, Chroma abre la cámara de tu móvil; la app recibe solo la foto que haces. Para usar una foto que ya tienes, el selector de fotos del sistema le entrega a la app solo la que eliges, nunca el resto de tu galería. De esa foto la app lee la fecha en que se hizo, para comprobar que es de hoy, y nada más.

La copia de seguridad de tu propio móvil

Tu móvil puede incluir Chroma en la copia que hace a tu propia cuenta en la nube. En Android, Chroma solo deja entrar su fichero de colores en la copia de Google cuando esa copia va cifrada de extremo a extremo, lo que exige tener bloqueo de pantalla; las fotos se quedan fuera de la copia en la nube y solo viajan cuando pasas los datos directamente de un móvil a otro. En iPhone y iPad, los datos de la app forman parte de la copia de iCloud del dispositivo si la tienes activada. Esas copias pertenecen a tu cuenta de Google o de Apple y se rigen por sus políticas. Nosotros no podemos leerlas.

Recordatorios

Si activas el recordatorio diario, la notificación se genera en tu dispositivo y siempre dice lo mismo: pregunta de qué color es hoy. Nunca enseña tus fotos, tus colores ni tus palabras.

Lo que sí sale de tu dispositivo

Compras

Chroma ofrece una única compra dentro de la aplicación, de pago único. El pago lo procesa la tienda desde la que instalaste Chroma, Google Play o la App Store; nosotros no vemos ni recibimos los datos de tu tarjeta, tu dirección de facturación ni ningún otro dato de pago.

Para saber si una instalación concreta ha comprado Chroma Pro, la app usa RevenueCat, Inc. como encargado del tratamiento. RevenueCat asigna a cada instalación un identificador anónimo y guarda, por cuenta nuestra, ese identificador, el historial de compras asociado e identificadores técnicos del dispositivo. Como Chroma no tiene cuentas, ese identificador no está vinculado a tu nombre, tu correo ni ningún perfil. Nada de tus fotos ni de tus colores se envía nunca a RevenueCat. Consulta la política de privacidad de RevenueCat.

RevenueCat trata los datos en Estados Unidos. Las transferencias fuera del Espacio Económico Europeo están amparadas por las Cláusulas Contractuales Tipo de la Comisión Europea.

Nada más

Chroma no incluye ningún SDK de publicidad, de analítica ni de informes de fallos. No elaboramos perfiles, no usamos remarketing y no vendemos ni compartimos información personal con nadie. La app necesita conexión a internet únicamente para comprobar el estado de tu compra.

Tú eliges dónde se guarda un fichero o a quién se manda una imagen. Una vez guardado o enviado queda fuera de nuestro control, y si va a una carpeta en la nube o a otra app, se le aplica la política de ese proveedor, no esta.

Cuánto tiempo se conservan los datos

Los datos de tu dispositivo se conservan hasta que borras un día o desinstalas la app. Los registros de compra que guardan RevenueCat y la tienda se conservan el tiempo que esos servicios necesiten para poder restaurar compras y para cumplir sus propias obligaciones legales y fiscales.
