Chroma Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

English - Español

The short version. Chroma turns one photo a day into one color, and your year of colors lives on your phone. Without an account, your photos, your colors, your words and your settings are stored only on your device and are never uploaded to us; the only data that leaves it is what is needed to know whether you have bought Chroma Pro. Friends is optional and needs an account: only if you turn it on, your visible name and the days you choose to share go to our server so that the friends you accepted can see them. Shared photos are deleted from the server after 7 days. There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind.

Who is responsible

Chroma ("the app") is published by Baltasar Jiménez ("we", "us"), an individual developer based in Spain, who acts as data controller for the limited processing described below.

Contact for anything in this policy: baltajmn@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Everything you create in Chroma is stored locally, in the app's private storage:

  • Your days: the date, the color you picked, the other colors the photo offered, the name of the color, and the optional word you added.
  • The photo of each day, as a reduced copy. The copy is made again from the pixels, so it carries none of the original file's metadata: no location, no camera details.
  • Your settings: reminder time, whether shared cards carry the "Chroma" mark, the date of your last backup, and whether you have purchased Chroma Pro.

The home screen widgets read a small separate file with today's color and its name, with Chroma Pro the colors of this year's days and, if you use Friends, the colors your friends shared today, without their names. It never contains photos, words or names.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else, except the days you choose to share with Friends, described below. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

The camera and your photos

To take the photo of the day, Chroma opens your phone's camera; the app receives only the picture you take. To use a photo you already have, the system photo picker hands the app only the photo you choose, and never the rest of your library. From that photo the app reads the date it was taken, to check that it is from today, and nothing else.

Your phone's own backup

Your phone may include Chroma in the backup it makes to your own cloud account. On Android, Chroma only allows its file of colors into Google's backup when that backup is end-to-end encrypted, which requires a screen lock on your phone; photos are left out of the cloud backup and only travel when you transfer directly from one phone to another. On iPhone and iPad, the app's data is part of your device's iCloud Backup if you have it turned on. Those backups belong to your Google or Apple account and are covered by their policies. We cannot read them.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, the notification is created on your device and always says the same thing: it asks what color today is. It never shows your photos, your colors or your words.

What leaves your device

Purchases

Chroma offers a single one-time in-app purchase. Payments are processed by the store you installed Chroma from, Google Play or the App Store; we never see or receive your card details, billing address or any other payment information.

To know whether a given installation has bought Chroma Pro, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc. as a processor. RevenueCat assigns each installation an anonymous identifier and stores, on our behalf, that identifier, the purchase history associated with it, and technical device identifiers. This identifier is never linked to your name, your email address or your Friends account, if you have one. Nothing from your photos or your colors is ever sent to RevenueCat. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

RevenueCat processes data in the United States. Transfers outside the European Economic Area are covered by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Friends (optional)

Friends lets people who already know each other see each other's colors. It is off until you open it and sign in, and everything below applies only if you do.

  • Your account. You sign in with Apple or Google. We receive from them an account identifier and an email address (with Apple, it can be a private relay address), and we store them with the dates you signed in. We use the email only to identify your account if you ask us to delete it or contact us; we never write to you otherwise.
  • Your profile. The name your friends see, which you choose and can change, and a random invite code for your link. You confirm you are 16 or older; we do not store your age or birth date.
  • What you share. Each day is private unless you say otherwise. For a day you share, the server keeps its date, color, color name, your word if there is one, and the time of its last change. If you share it with the photo, a reduced copy (at most 720 pixels on the long side, made again from the pixels, with no location or camera metadata) is kept for 7 days and then deleted automatically. Only the friends you accepted can see it: there are no public profiles and no search.
  • Your circle. Who you are friends with, open requests, and the people you blocked.
  • Reports. If you report a friend's card, we store who reported, whose card and which day, and we receive it by email with the reported content so that we can act within 24 hours. The person reported is never told who reported them.

Friends runs on Supabase, Inc. as our processor, with the data stored in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). Report emails are delivered by Resend, Inc., which processes them in the United States under the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. See Supabase's privacy policy and Resend's privacy policy.

You can delete your account at any time in Settings > Friends > Delete account, or as explained at color.baltajmn.dev/delete. That erases your profile, your friendships, everything you shared and its photos, and the reports you made. Your journal on the phone is not touched.

Nothing else

Chroma contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting SDK. We do not build profiles, we do not use remarketing, and we do not sell or share personal information with anyone. The app needs an internet connection only to check your purchase status and, if you use Friends, to reach our server.

Files you export and cards you share

Chroma can export all your days as one file that includes your colors, words and photos. Cards and posters are images made on your device, and a card can be shared with the photo or with the color only. You choose where a file is saved or where an image is sent. Once saved or sent, it is outside our control, and if it goes to a cloud folder or to another app, that provider's policy applies to it, not this one.

How long data is kept

Data on your device is kept until you delete a day or uninstall the app. With Friends, a shared photo is kept 7 days; a shared day without its photo, until you make it private, delete it, or delete your account; your profile and friendships, until you delete your account; a report, until the account of whoever made it or of whoever was reported is deleted. Purchase records held by RevenueCat and by the app store are kept for as long as those services require in order to honour restores and to meet their own legal and tax obligations.

Children

Chroma is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Friends requires being 16 or older. There is no public content: what is shared is seen only by friends who were invited by link and accepted.

Your rights under the GDPR

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and object to the processing of your personal data, and the right to data portability.

In practice, most of these you can exercise yourself and instantly: the data is on your phone. Deleting a day erases it; uninstalling the app erases everything; the export feature is data portability. With Friends, you can change your name, make any day private, and delete your account from the app. For a copy of what the server holds about you, or for the purchase data held by RevenueCat, write to baltajmn@gmail.com and we will act on your request. We can only identify your records if you provide the Google Play or App Store order number, precisely because we hold nothing that identifies you.

Legal bases: performance of a contract (delivering the purchase you made, and the Friends service you signed up for) and our legitimate interest in preventing fraudulent restores and in keeping Friends safe through reports. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority. In Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos.

Your rights under the CCPA / CPRA

If you are a California resident: in the last twelve months we have collected the categories "identifiers" (an anonymous installation identifier and device identifiers and, with Friends, your account identifier and email address), "commercial information" (your purchase history) and, with Friends, the content you chose to share, for the sole purpose of delivering the product and the service you asked for.

We have not sold or shared personal information, and we do not use it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We do not knowingly sell the personal information of anyone under 16. You have the right to know, to delete, to correct and to non-discrimination for exercising those rights; write to baltajmn@gmail.com.

Do Not Track

Chroma does not track you across apps or websites, so there is no tracking behaviour to change in response to a Do Not Track signal.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date at the top, and material changes will also be described in the app's release notes in the store. The terms of use of Friends are at color.baltajmn.dev/terms.html.

English - Español

En corto. Chroma convierte una foto al día en un color, y tu año de colores vive en tu móvil. Sin cuenta, tus fotos, tus colores, tus palabras y tus ajustes se guardan solo en tu dispositivo y no se nos envían nunca; lo único que sale es lo necesario para saber si has comprado Chroma Pro. Amigos es opcional y necesita cuenta: solo si lo activas, tu nombre visible y los días que decidas compartir van a nuestro servidor para que los vean los amigos que has aceptado. Las fotos compartidas se borran del servidor a los 7 días. No hay publicidad, ni analítica, ni seguimiento de ningún tipo.

Quién es el responsable

Chroma ("la app") está publicada por Baltasar Jiménez ("nosotros"), desarrollador individual con residencia en España, que actúa como responsable del tratamiento limitado que se describe abajo.

Contacto para cualquier asunto de esta política: baltajmn@gmail.com.

Lo que se queda en tu dispositivo

Todo lo que creas en Chroma se guarda en local, en el almacenamiento privado de la app:

  • Tus días: la fecha, el color que elegiste, los otros colores que ofrecía la foto, el nombre del color y la palabra opcional que añadiste.
  • La foto de cada día, como copia reducida. La copia se hace de nuevo a partir de los píxeles, así que no lleva ninguno de los metadatos del fichero original: ni ubicación, ni datos de la cámara.
  • Tus ajustes: hora del recordatorio, si las tarjetas llevan la marca "Chroma", la fecha de tu última copia y si has comprado Chroma Pro.

Los widgets de inicio leen un fichero aparte, pequeño, con el color de hoy y su nombre, con Chroma Pro los colores de los días de este año y, si usas Amigos, los colores que tus amigos compartieron hoy, sin sus nombres. Nunca contiene fotos, palabras ni nombres.

Nada de esto se nos envía ni se envía a terceros, salvo los días que decidas compartir en Amigos, como se explica abajo. Desinstalar la app lo borra.

La cámara y tus fotos

Para hacer la foto del día, Chroma abre la cámara de tu móvil; la app recibe solo la foto que haces. Para usar una foto que ya tienes, el selector de fotos del sistema le entrega a la app solo la que eliges, nunca el resto de tu galería. De esa foto la app lee la fecha en que se hizo, para comprobar que es de hoy, y nada más.

La copia de seguridad de tu propio móvil

Tu móvil puede incluir Chroma en la copia que hace a tu propia cuenta en la nube. En Android, Chroma solo deja entrar su fichero de colores en la copia de Google cuando esa copia va cifrada de extremo a extremo, lo que exige tener bloqueo de pantalla; las fotos se quedan fuera de la copia en la nube y solo viajan cuando pasas los datos directamente de un móvil a otro. En iPhone y iPad, los datos de la app forman parte de la copia de iCloud del dispositivo si la tienes activada. Esas copias pertenecen a tu cuenta de Google o de Apple y se rigen por sus políticas. Nosotros no podemos leerlas.

Recordatorios

Si activas el recordatorio diario, la notificación se genera en tu dispositivo y siempre dice lo mismo: pregunta de qué color es hoy. Nunca enseña tus fotos, tus colores ni tus palabras.

Lo que sí sale de tu dispositivo

Compras

Chroma ofrece una única compra dentro de la aplicación, de pago único. El pago lo procesa la tienda desde la que instalaste Chroma, Google Play o la App Store; nosotros no vemos ni recibimos los datos de tu tarjeta, tu dirección de facturación ni ningún otro dato de pago.

Para saber si una instalación concreta ha comprado Chroma Pro, la app usa RevenueCat, Inc. como encargado del tratamiento. RevenueCat asigna a cada instalación un identificador anónimo y guarda, por cuenta nuestra, ese identificador, el historial de compras asociado e identificadores técnicos del dispositivo. Ese identificador nunca se vincula a tu nombre, tu correo ni a tu cuenta de Amigos, si la tienes. Nada de tus fotos ni de tus colores se envía nunca a RevenueCat. Consulta la política de privacidad de RevenueCat.

RevenueCat trata los datos en Estados Unidos. Las transferencias fuera del Espacio Económico Europeo están amparadas por las Cláusulas Contractuales Tipo de la Comisión Europea.

Amigos (opcional)

Amigos deja que gente que ya se conoce vea sus colores. Está apagado hasta que lo abres e inicias sesión, y todo lo que sigue se aplica solo si lo haces.

  • Tu cuenta. Entras con Apple o con Google. De ellos recibimos un identificador de cuenta y un correo (con Apple puede ser una dirección de reenvío privada), y los guardamos con las fechas en que entraste. El correo solo lo usamos para identificar tu cuenta si nos pides borrarla o nos escribes; nunca te escribimos por otra cosa.
  • Tu perfil. El nombre que ven tus amigos, que eliges tú y puedes cambiar, y un código de invitación al azar para tu enlace. Confirmas que tienes 16 años o más; no guardamos tu edad ni tu fecha de nacimiento.
  • Lo que compartes. Cada día es privado salvo que digas lo contrario. De un día que compartes, el servidor guarda su fecha, su color, el nombre del color, tu palabra si la hay y la hora de su último cambio. Si lo compartes con la foto, se guarda una copia reducida (720 píxeles como mucho en el lado largo, hecha de nuevo a partir de los píxeles, sin ubicación ni datos de la cámara) durante 7 días, y después se borra sola. Solo la ven los amigos que aceptaste: no hay perfiles públicos ni buscador.
  • Tu círculo. Con quién eres amigo, las solicitudes abiertas y las personas que bloqueaste.
  • Reportes. Si reportas la tarjeta de un amigo, guardamos quién reporta, de quién es la tarjeta y qué día, y nos llega por correo con el contenido reportado para poder actuar en menos de 24 horas. A la persona reportada nunca se le dice quién la reportó.

Amigos funciona sobre Supabase, Inc., como encargado del tratamiento, con los datos guardados en la Unión Europea (Fráncfort, Alemania). Los correos de los reportes los entrega Resend, Inc., que los trata en Estados Unidos al amparo de las Cláusulas Contractuales Tipo de la Comisión Europea. Consulta la política de privacidad de Supabase y la de Resend.

Puedes borrar tu cuenta cuando quieras en Ajustes > Amigos > Borrar cuenta, o como se explica en color.baltajmn.dev/delete. Eso borra tu perfil, tus amistades, todo lo que compartiste y sus fotos, y los reportes que hiciste. Tu diario del móvil no se toca.

Nada más

Chroma no incluye ningún SDK de publicidad, de analítica ni de informes de fallos. No elaboramos perfiles, no usamos remarketing y no vendemos ni compartimos información personal con nadie. La app necesita conexión a internet solo para comprobar el estado de tu compra y, si usas Amigos, para llegar a nuestro servidor.

Una vez guardado o enviado queda fuera de nuestro control, y si va a una carpeta en la nube o a otra app, se le aplica la política de ese proveedor, no esta.

Cuánto tiempo se conservan los datos

Los datos de tu dispositivo se conservan hasta que borras un día o desinstalas la app. Con Amigos, una foto compartida se guarda 7 días; un día compartido sin su foto, hasta que lo haces privado, lo borras o borras tu cuenta; tu perfil y tus amistades, hasta que borras tu cuenta; un reporte, hasta que se borra la cuenta de quien lo hizo o de quien fue reportado. Los registros de compra que guardan RevenueCat y la tienda se conservan el tiempo que esos servicios necesiten para poder restaurar compras y para cumplir sus propias obligaciones legales y fiscales.
